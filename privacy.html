Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how PuzzlePanch handles information when you visit and use this website.

Overview

PuzzlePanch offers browser-based puzzle games, including Sudoku, 2048, Memory Match, Sliding Puzzle, and Tic-Tac-Toe.

Game information

The website may store game progress, scores, settings, and preferences locally in your browser so the games can work as intended. No account is required to play. You can clear this information using your browser’s site-data settings.

Advertising and cookies

PuzzlePanch includes a Google AdSense script. If advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted, personalize them. Learn more from Google’s advertising technologies information. A privacy policy does not replace any legally required consent mechanism.

Who operates this website

This website is maintained as part of the QELZAVRYN collection. For questions about this policy or the website, email contact.qelzavryn@gmail.com.

Hosting and technical information

When you visit the website, hosting and network providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, requested pages, and access timestamps to deliver the site, maintain security, and diagnose issues. This is separate from information you deliberately enter into the website.

Contacting us

If you send us an email, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your message and manage correspondence. Please do not send passwords, bank details, or other sensitive information by email.

Your choices

You can control cookies and locally stored site data in your browser settings. Clearing site data may also remove saved progress or records. You may contact us to ask about information you have emailed us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the website or the services it uses change. The revised version will be posted on this page.
