How to play Tic-Tac-Toe

Place X or O in an empty square. Three in a row wins; choose computer or same-device friend.

Try this strategy

Take the center when available and block an opponent with two marks in a line.

Use the game toolbar to restart or start a new round. Your browser may retain game progress locally.
